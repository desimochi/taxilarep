    1. Pedagogical Intent & Triple-Threat Economics

    The StratSim TBS simulation represents a pinnacle of management education at Taxila Business School. It is designed to immerse the executive student in a high-fidelity mathematical model where market share is not earned through raw spending, but through the calibrated alignment of technical utility and price elasticity. Participants are forced to move beyond static textbook definitions of the 4Ps to experience the "Friction of Competitive Warfare." You are now competing against two high-intelligence AI firms, BetaCorp and GammaCorp, both designed to maintain absolute mathematical dominance.

    A. Dynamic Positioning Science: Executives will learn that consumer demand is a dynamic function of "Spec Distance." Using the R&D Lab, you must predict the drift of segment "Ideal Points" and launch products that hit these points with precision. You will internalize that yesterday's spec-leader is today's legacy burden. Technical utility decays at a rate of 15% per annum, requiring constant engineering reinvestment.

    B. Macro-Environmental Survivability: The world of Taxila is volatile. Stochastically generated shocks—such as Trade Embargos (+40% COGS) and Deep Recessions (-45% Demand)—will test your firm's break-even resilience. Learning to maintain positive net contribution during a liquidity crisis is a prerequisite for graduating from this simulation. One macro-economic event is guaranteed every quarter to ensure constant strategic pivoting.

    C. Capital Efficiency & The Inventory Sinkhole: With an initial liquid cash cap of $100,000, every dollar is a finite resource. You will learn that excess inventory is not just "unsold goods"—it is "Dead Capital" that incurs an 18% quarterly penalty. Mastering the logistical alignment between production and the 30% demand ceiling is the difference between an Apex SPI and corporate termination. Overproduction is the most common cause of corporate bankruptcy in the simulation.

    2. Regulatory Guardrails & Antitrust Compliance

    To ensure a rigorous and fair market simulation, the Board has implemented mandatory regulatory guardrails. If your strategic plan violates these rules, the "Validation Auditor" will reject your turn submission and require a complete revision of your numbers:

    I. The 8% Advertising Budget Cap: In the interest of sustainable growth and budget discipline, the marketing spend for any single brand is strictly capped at 8% of the firm's total quarterly liquidity. This mandate forces executives to prioritize product innovation (R&D) over raw media noise.

    II. The 30% Market Share Ceiling: Under strict antitrust legislation, no player brand is permitted to fulfill more than 30% of total segment demand. If your product's utility is high enough to capture 50%, the system will forcibly cap your sales at 30%. This rule necessitates a diversified portfolio strategy—executives must target multiple segments to grow total firm revenue.

    III. Price Regulatory Oversight: Retail prices are capped at $1,500 or 5x the Unit Manufacturing Cost. This regulatory ceiling prevents predatory gouging and ensures that competition remains focused on technical innovation and efficiency.

    3. The Innovation Mandate: Continuous R&D

    Stagnation is a terminable offense. The Taxila economy is defined by rapid technical obsolescence. To simulate this pressure, the following R&D rules are in effect:

    Quarterly R&D Sequence: Executives are required to launch at least one new R&D project every 2nd quarter (Q2, Q4, etc.). If you attempt to advance these periods without a project, the turn will be blocked. Furthermore, should a firm fail to initiate any R&D project for four consecutive quarters, the CEO is immediately removed for "Strategic Inertia," resulting in an instant Game Over.

    Product Optimization: Each R&D project costs $5,000. When launching, you must refer to the "Consumer Ideal Points" table. Target specs that are at least 2-3 points ahead of current ideals to account for the lead-time between project launch and market availability.

    4. Performance Graduation & Post-Sim Audit

    Quarter 12 marks the end of your executive contract. Upon completion, the system executes a final forensic audit. You will be required to download your Executive Decision Log (.csv) and answer five justification questions regarding your pricing logic, inventory management, and R&D sequence. Only after providing this academic reflection will the system generate your verifiable Certificate of Strategic Excellence, signed by Dean Prof. Rajat Bohra.
