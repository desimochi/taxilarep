Simulation Guide

Objective

Survive for 20 Quarters and maximize your Cumulative Sales. Avoid bankruptcy (2 consecutive quarters with zero sales).

Core Functions

  • Price: Set the unit price (₹48k - ₹64k). Each consumer segment has a maximum price they are willing to pay. Exceeding it results in 0 sales from that segment.
  • Marketing Budget: Spending here increases your Brand Awareness. Higher awareness attracts more customers, especially from "Mainstream" and "Aspirational" segments.
  • R&D Investment: Spending here increases your Technology Level and Product Quality. Tech Enthusiasts and Savvy Shoppers demand high quality.

Ad Campaigns

You must run one campaign per quarter.

  • Targeting: Choose one specific segment to target.
  • Message: Write a slogan. Use keywords like "cheap", "affordable", "value" for price-sensitive buyers, or "premium", "best", "quality" for quality-focused buyers.
  • Warning: Misleading ads (e.g., claiming "cheap" when price is high > ₹56k, or "quality" when quality is low < 70) will severely penalize sales.

Consumer Segments

Budget Buyers: Max Price ₹56k. Care about Price.
Savvy Shoppers: Max Price ₹60k. Care about Value (Price + Quality).
Aspirational: Max Price ₹64k. Care about Awareness/Status.
Tech Enthusiasts: No Price Limit. Demand High Quality (>75).

Game Rules

  • Time Limit: You have 5 minutes per quarter. If time runs out, the game ends.
  • Budget: Your budget for the next quarter is 12% of your cumulative sales. Spend wisely!
