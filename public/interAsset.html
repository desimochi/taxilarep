Simulation Briefing

Objective

The modern global economy is deeply intertwined. The objective of this simulation is to help policymakers and analysts predict the cascading effects of a localized economic "shock." By observing how changes in one primary asset impact others, you will develop a rigorous understanding of macroeconomic elasticities, import inflation, and currency dynamics.

How to Play

  1. Shock Simulator: Select an asset, apply a % shock, and observe the deterministic impact on India's economy based on historical betas.
  2. Historical Analysis: Navigate to the Historical Data tab to cross-reference actual macro data from the last 3 years to visually prove these correlations.
  3. Live Exchange: Trade the Live MCX simulator. Understand Margin limits (MIS vs NRML), set Stop Losses, and manage your portfolio through surprise geopolitical events.

Glossary of Terms

US Dollar Index (DXY)
A measure of the value of the U.S. dollar relative to a basket of foreign currencies. A strong DXY often pulls capital out of emerging markets.
Crude Oil (Brent)
The global benchmark for oil prices. Highly critical for India, as it imports ~85% of its crude, directly impacting the Current Account Deficit (CAD).
USD/INR Exchange Rate
The amount of Indian Rupees required to buy one US Dollar. When this rate goes up, the Rupee is depreciating (weakening).
Intraday (MIS) vs Normal (NRML)
MIS provides 5x leverage but the order is automatically squared off at 11:30 PM real-world time. NRML requires full capital but can be held overnight.

Trading Guidelines & Winning Strategy

1. The Core Objective

To score points, you must survive for a minimum of 30 simulated days and outperform the market. The scoring is brutally exponential: achieving a score of 70 requires ~35% ROI, 80 requires ~65% ROI, and 90+ requires an elite ~110% ROI. A 25% portfolio loss instantly drops your score to 0.

2. The Winning Formula (How to Win)

  • Read the DEPR Intelligence Desk: The daily briefing predicts the immediate future. If it says Oil is expected to drop 5%, you must understand the ripple effects immediately.
  • Utilize Leverage (MIS) Wisely: Intraday (MIS) orders give you 5x margin. A 2% market move becomes a 10% portfolio gain. To reach a score of 80+, you must utilize MIS during high-conviction macroeconomic events.
  • Always Use Stop Losses: Because MIS amplifies losses 5x, a sudden geopolitical shock can wipe you out in seconds. Always set a Stop Loss (SL) to protect your capital.

3. The Macro Matrix (Cheat Sheet)

Use these established elasticities to predict secondary asset movements:

📈 DXY (USD) Goes UP:
➡️ Gold & Silver Drop, INR Weakens, Nifty Drops
📈 Crude Oil Goes UP:
➡️ INR Weakens (CAD expands), Nifty Drops
📉 Nifty Goes UP:
➡️ INR Strengthens (FDI inflows)
⚠️ War/Risk-Off Event:
➡️ Gold Surges, DXY Surges, Nifty Crashes

4. Import Parity Rule (Crucial)

Gold and Silver are priced internationally in USD, but you are trading them in INR. If global Gold drops 2%, but the Rupee depreciates against the Dollar by 2% at the same time, the domestic price of Gold in India will remain flat. Trade the Net India Impact, not just the global headline.
