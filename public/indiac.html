Mission Objectives

Player's Manual & Learning Outcomes

1. The Objective

You are the CMO. Your goal is to launch 10 randomly selected products across India. You must maximize Net Profit and Brand Impact while avoiding cultural pitfalls.

2. Core Mechanics

  • Targeting: Select up to 5 states per launch. You cannot re-select a state immediately (2-turn cooldown).
  • Budgeting: Marketing costs scale with population. Launching in UP (230M people) costs 100x more than Goa (1.5M). Manage your funds!
  • Creative: Write a punchline in English. Select a target language. The AI translates and grades it.

3. The Logic Engine

  • Family Veto: The Family Buying Unit (Dadi, Father, Mother, Son) can veto your sale. E.g., Selling meat to a vegetarian household = Dadi Veto.
  • Linguistic Impact: Native language ads get 100% effectiveness. Hindi is 90% effective in North, but suffers in the South. Mismatched languages result in 0 sales.
  • Environment: Selling winter wear in tropical Kerala fails. Luxury goods in low-income states fail without discounts.

4. Learning Outcomes

  • Cultural Intelligence: Understanding India is 28+ diverse micro-markets.
  • Economic Geography: Correlating population, income, and cost.
  • Strategic Resource Allocation: Prioritizing high-ROI markets.
