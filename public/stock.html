GUIDE Terminal Manual

1. Fund Management

  • Bank vs Margin: You start with ₹10 Lakhs in your Bank. You CANNOT trade with Bank balance directly. You must transfer funds to your Margin account first.
  • Transfer Button: Located at the top right. Use it to:
    • D (Deposit): Move money Bank -> Margin.
    • W (Withdraw): Move profit Margin -> Bank.
    • A (Add Cash): Simulate adding external capital.

2. Placing Orders

Product Types

  • CNC (Delivery): Buy stock to hold. Requires 100% margin. Sell anytime after purchase.
  • MIS (Intraday): Buy/Sell for same day. 5x Leverage provided (e.g., ₹10k margin buys ₹50k stock).

Order Types

  • MARKET: Execute immediately at current price (LTP).
  • LIMIT: Execute only at your specified price (or better).
  • SL-M: Stop Loss Market. Triggers a market order when price hits trigger.

3. Analysis & Charts

  • Charts: View candlestick history in 1D, 5D, 1M, 6M, 1Y, 5Y timeframes. Data dynamically connects to live prices.
